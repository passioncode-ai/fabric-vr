# Axis B — UI, interaction, reachability

Read at `feat/v1-notes-core` @ `b32af38`. Everything below is from source or from the Spatial SDK
0.14.0 binaries in `~/.gradle/caches/modules-2/files-2.1/com.meta.spatial/` (disassembled with
`javap`; the SDK ships no sources). Nothing was built or run — every "on device" claim is marked as
needing a device.

Two SDK facts decided most of this axis, and neither is in the repo's comments:

1. `composePanel` (meta-spatial-sdk-compose 0.14.0 → `PanelRegistrationExtensionKt.composePanel`)
   creates the `ComposeView` and then calls `AppSystemActivityExtensionKt.attachLifecycleToRootView`,
   which sets `ViewTreeLifecycleOwner`, `ViewTreeViewModelStoreOwner` and
   `ViewTreeSavedStateRegistryOwner` from `ComposeFeature.panelViewLifecycleOwner`. **Three of the
   four owners `SpatialPanelOwners` supplies were already there.** Only
   `OnBackPressedDispatcherOwner` was genuinely missing — which is why the crash was real and why
   the fix works, but the file's premise ("a panel it registers has none of them") is wrong.
2. A Spatial panel is an `android.app.Presentation` on a `VirtualDisplay`
   (`PanelDisplayBase.initVirtualDisplay`, `createVirtualDisplay("vr", w, h, dpi, surface, 11)`),
   and `PanelDisplayBase.dispatchEvent` **returns without dispatching** whenever
   `imm.isAcceptingText()` is true. That single early-return is B-01.

## Notes for the merge

- **Two of the three Blockers are cheap.** B-02 is a decision (build *New note* + the today card, or
  cut them from three documents); B-03 is a two-line test fix. Neither should be deferred, because
  together they are why nobody noticed that the app cannot create a text note.
- **B-01 is the one that decides whether the Space ships.** Everything else in the Space degrades;
  this one traps the person inside a screen. Test it first on a headset — before any of the polish
  rows — because if the IME behaves that way, the Space needs a different text-entry design, not a
  patch, and several rows below it (B-27, B-34, parts of B-09) fold into that redesign.
- **B-09 and B-01 push the same way:** the screen's own doctrine (`TodayScreen.kt:63-72`) is that a
  second window in a headset is a wall. A `Popup` is a second window and the IME is a second window.
  The one place the product breaks its own rule is the one place its host is least likely to
  cooperate. Replacing the `DropdownMenu` with inline content removes a device-unknown from the
  matrix at the cost of a few lines.
- **`SpatialPanelOwners` should shrink, not grow** (B-16). Its doc says the next missing owner would
  otherwise be discovered by a crash; in fact three of the four were never missing, and the file now
  maintains a second lifecycle that the SDK's own `ComposeFeature` already maintains correctly. Keep
  the back dispatcher, delete the rest, and fix the comment — the comment is currently the most
  authoritative-sounding wrong statement in the repository.
- **Nine of the 26 unused strings are finished copy for features that are wired everywhere except
  the last line of UI** (B-05, B-10, B-19, B-21). Those are the cheapest user-visible wins in this
  report: the words exist, the state exists, only the `Text(...)` is missing.
- **Order I would take them in:** B-01 (measure) → B-02 → B-03 → B-06, B-04, B-05 (first-session
  paths that mislead) → B-08, B-11 (geometry and layout, both arithmetic and both cheap) → B-12,
  B-10 → the rest.
