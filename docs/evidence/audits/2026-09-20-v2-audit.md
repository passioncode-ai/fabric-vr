each other, then merged here. The raw reports are `docs/evidence/audits/2026-09-20-v2/{A..F}.md` and
they are kept whole: this file is the register, not a replacement.

## What this audit found that the last one could not

The first audit (2026-09-19) read a product whose main path had never run. This one reads a product
that runs, on two headsets, after a day of changes made under a person's direct feedback. The
character of the findings changed with it, and three patterns account for most of the severity.

**The screen rewrite took working features with it.** Replacing the capture sheet and the hold
gesture on 2026-09-19/20 was right — the operator could not use either — but `TodayScreen` was
rewritten rather than edited, and everything that was not re-typed is gone while its view-model
half remains. `createNote()`, `selectTag()`, the date header, today's card, the tag chips, and 23
string resources are implemented, tested in some cases, and called by nothing. **A text note cannot
be created in either host.** Four axes found this independently (A-01, B-02, D-01, E).

**The app tells the person the opposite of what it does, twice.** On a failed transcription the only
button is *Retry*, wired to `VoiceViewModel.cancel()`, which deletes the recording — directly under
a message that reads "The recording is kept." The correct `retryLast()` exists and is never called.
And *Undo* after a delete restores the database row while the vault has already removed the `.wav`,
so the note returns pointing at a file that is gone and *Transcribe again* answers with a
`FileNotFoundException`. Both are data loss, both are reachable in one tap, and both are new.

**Two seams are wrong at the platform level, not in our logic.** `NetworkPolicy` reads a URL's
userinfo as its host, so `http://192.168.0.5:8080@evil.invalid/` passes the private-address test
while OkHttp resolves the public host — the cloud Bearer key and a WAV of the person's voice travel
in the clear to wherever the attacker named. And the Spatial SDK's `PanelDisplayBase.dispatchEvent`
returns without dispatching whenever the software keyboard is accepting text, so with an IME up **no
tap reaches the panel at all**; `SearchScreen` focuses its field on entry, and nothing in the app
ever hides the keyboard. In the Space, Search is a trap with no way out.

report of it:

- **The *Retry* that deletes** — A-03, B-06, D.

## What the later axes added that changes the shape of the work

- **Russian search does not work, measured.** `@Fts4` takes Room's default `simple` tokenizer, which
  case-folds ASCII only. Reproduced against sqlite3: a table containing `панель` returns 0 rows for
  `MATCH 'Панель*'` and 1 for `unicode61`. Whisper capitalises the opening of every sentence, so
  this is the common case, not an edge (G-04).
- **The person cannot get their notes out.** The vault is `filesDir/vault` with
  `allowBackup="false"`, no SAF picker, no `FileProvider`, no share intent. The product definition's
  "notes you own, Obsidian-compatible" is false on this build (G-01) — and the next planned step,
  a signed release, requires `adb uninstall`, which deletes `filesDir` entirely (H-23). **An export
  path must ship before any signed install, not after.**
- **A voice archive nobody was told about.** A `.wav` per dictation, kept forever, with no player,
  no size shown, no per-recording delete, and no interface string that mentions audio at all —
  about 1 MB per 30 seconds, roughly 7 GB a year at twenty dictations a day (G-02, G-16).
- **Nothing would tell anyone that a build is failing.** No crash capture, no log sink beyond a
  logcat ring that clears on reboot, no CI. The one production crash in this project's history was
  found by a person running `adb shell dumpsys activity exit-info` by hand (H-01, H-08).
- **The SDK cannot be upgraded out of the keyboard trap.** 0.14.0 is the newest that exists, so
  B-01 needs a workaround in our code rather than a version bump.

## A correction the spec pass made to this audit

Axis D graded the scenarios 3 holding, 9 drifted, 3 broken. SCN-011 ("take the notes as files") was
graded *holds*; G-01 settles that the vault is unreachable — no picker, no provider, no share, and
`allowBackup="false"` — so the scenario cannot be performed at all. The real score is
**2 holding, 10 drifted, 3 broken**.

## What no reader could settle

- Whether the IME appears at all for a Spatial panel, and what the SDK offers instead. B-01 proves
  taps are swallowed while it is up; it does not prove the keyboard can be raised in the first
  place. **This decides whether six scenarios are panel-only**, so it is the first thing to measure
  on the headset.
- On-head legibility and hit rate at the distance people actually sit.
- Transcription quality across voices and rooms.
- Whether the release APK runs: it has never been signed or installed.

## Where the reports disagreed, and how it was settled

`SpatialPanelOwners` supplies four owners. Axis B said three were already provided by the SDK; axis
C treated all four as ours. Axis I settled it by disassembly: `composePanel` builds the ComposeView
and calls `attachLifecycleToRootView`, which sets `ViewTreeLifecycleOwner`,
`ViewTreeViewModelStoreOwner` and `ViewTreeSavedStateRegistryOwner` on that view to
`ComposeFeature.panelViewLifecycleOwner` — an owner the activity actually drives
(`VrActivity.onStop` dispatches `FeatureManager.onStopActivity` at offset 125).

**Axis B was right.** Our `CompositionLocalProvider` shadows three working owners with three we
drive by hand and drive incompletely — ours never receives `ON_STOP` (I-12). Only
`OnBackPressedDispatcherOwner` was ever missing. The correct shape is to keep that one, delegating
its lifecycle to the SDK's owner, and drop the other three. That single change also removes I-03,
where `owners.onDestroy()` clears the ViewModelStore before the SDK disposes the composition, so
`onDispose { flush() }` launches into a dead scope — in the Space that is not a race, it is the
order on every exit.

Axis I also settled the back dispatcher itself: `VrActivity.dispatchKeyEvent` is 65 bytes and never
calls `super`, nothing calls `onBackPressedDispatcher.onBackPressed()`, and
`setOnBackInvokedDispatcher` is never registered. The dispatcher delivers zero events and
`returnToPanel()` is unreachable through it (I-13).

## Method, and what it cost

Six readers on one model, blind to each other, each with an axis brief naming what to read and what
to look for. Two waves: the first three axes (B, C, E) died on a model spend limit and were
relaunched; D, A and F completed on the first attempt. Every Blocker was then re-read by hand
against the code, and two claims — the SDK's touch dispatch and R8's name retention — were settled
by disassembling a jar rather than by reasoning about it.

The plan for this audit is `docs/evidence/audits/2026-09-20-v2-audit-plan.md`. Nothing in this pass
was fixed; the next pass turns these findings into tasks.
