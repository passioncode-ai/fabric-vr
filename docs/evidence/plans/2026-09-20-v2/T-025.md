## What is wrong, in the person's terms

Three things leave the headset, or leave the app. Each happens silently at the moment it happens.

**1. The assistant posts the person's notes to a US company.** Asking one question builds a context
of up to **12,000 characters** of note titles, bodies, transcripts, tags and dates
(`NotesContextBuilder.kt:27`, `:37-45`) and posts it to `openrouter.ai`, which routes it onward to
whichever provider serves `anthropic/claude-sonnet-5`. At the ~540 characters a rendered dictation
costs, that is around **22 notes per question**.

No string says so. The nearest is `chat_reading` — "reading: %1$s"
(`app/src/main/res/values/strings.xml:96`) — which is set from `ask.usedTitles` **after the request
is already in flight** (`ChatViewModel.kt:78`, after the `Graph.assistant.ask(...)` at `:72`), shows
at most four titles (`ChatScreen.kt:69`), and never says anything left the device.

The person taps a **microphone icon** in the header (`TodayScreen.kt:128` — G-15), lands on a screen
with three inviting example questions (`ChatScreen.kt:84-88`), taps one, and twenty-two of their
notes are on someone else's servers. `docs/ux/foundation.md` JTBD-03 names this exact anxiety: "my
notes leaving the device without me deciding."

**2. The recording goes to a cloud provider, and the screen that sends it does not say so.** This is
disclosed — but only in Settings, and only while you are standing on the choice:
`provider_local_note` / `provider_cloud_note` / `provider_server_note`
(`strings.xml:130-132`, rendered at `SettingsScreen.kt:140-144`). The **Today** screen, where the
recording is actually made, shows nothing: `RecordControl` (`TodayScreen.kt:240-344`) never mentions
the provider in any state. A person who set *Cloud service* in July presses Record in September with
no reminder that their voice is going anywhere.

**3. Every transcript is written to the system clipboard.** `TodayScreen.kt:104`,
`clipboard.setText(AnnotatedString(ready.transcript.text))`, on every dictation, unconditionally.
`DEC-0012` accepted this deliberately and its own *Consequences* line says it plainly: "The app
overwrites a cross-app resource the person did not offer, on every dictation, and the only notice
disappears after a few seconds." The notice is `state_saved_and_copied`, shown for
`CONFIRMATION_MS = 2_500L` (`TodayScreen.kt:169-175`, `:460`).

The app is careful about the **voice** and silent about the **text**. That is the wrong way round:
the text is the distilled version.

## The asymmetry, stated as a table

| What leaves | Where it goes | Disclosed where | Disclosed when | Can it be turned off |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| the recording | the endpoint in Settings | Settings, beside the choice | while choosing, never after | yes — *On this headset* |
| up to 12,000 chars of notes | openrouter.ai and onward | **nowhere** | **never** | only by not asking |
| every transcript | the system clipboard | one line for 2.5 s | at the moment, briefly | **no** |

## The decision, and what was weighed

**Chosen: four small disclosures and one switch. No consent framework, no dialogs, no first-run
privacy screen.**

This is a two-person app on two sideloaded headsets. A consent flow with an accept button would be
theatre, and worse, it is the kind of thing that makes a person click past the one sentence that
mattered. What is owed here is that **the truth is visible at the place and moment it applies**.

1. **A permanent line on the Chat screen's empty state**, above the example questions, in the same
   register as `provider_cloud_note`: *"Answers are produced by OpenRouter. Your notes are sent
   there to answer."*
2. **A line beside the OpenRouter key in Settings** (`SettingsScreen.kt:91-102`), matching the way
   speech already discloses at the point of choice.
3. **`chat_reading` becomes permanent and quantitative** — *"Sent %1$d of your %2$d notes"* — and is
   set **before** the request, not after. Today it is decoration; made honest it is the only number
   that tells the person how much of their base went.
4. **The provider on the Today screen when it is not local.** A one-line label under the record
   button whenever `sttProvider() != LOCAL`: *"This recording is sent to %1$s."* Nothing when it is
   local — an app that announces "nothing is leaving" every time trains people to stop reading.
5. **The clipboard becomes a setting.** One switch in Settings, *Copy the transcript to the
   clipboard*, default **on** (DEC-0012 stands; this does not overturn it). The existing
   `state_saved_and_copied` already tells the truth; what is missing is the ability to say no.

**Alternatives considered:**

- *A first-run privacy screen listing all three.* Rejected: it is read once, before any of the three
  has ever happened, and it directly fights T-031, whose job is to get a person to a first dictation
  without reading a document.
- *A confirmation dialog before each assistant question.* Rejected: it would fire on every question,
  so it would be dismissed reflexively within a day, and in a headset a dialog is another thing to
  aim at (the reasoning `TodayScreen.kt:63-72` already gives for having no sheets).
- *Per-note exclusion from the assistant's context*, as G-03 suggests as an option. Rejected for v1:
  it needs a new column, an editor control and a rule for what the assistant says when the answer
  was in an excluded note. It is a real feature, not a disclosure, and it is the wrong shape while
  T-033 may cut the screen entirely.
- *Making the assistant opt-in behind a setting.* Rejected as redundant: it is already opt-in by
  construction — no OpenRouter key, no request (`AppError.NoApiKey` →
  `error_no_api_key`, `UiStateMapper.kt:53-54`). Adding a second gate in front of a gate is noise.
- *Stop copying to the clipboard by default.* Rejected: DEC-0012's reasoning holds — dictating in a
  headset is usually done to paste somewhere else, and taking the default away costs the product its
  best moment. A switch is enough.
- *Also disclose that the model download reaches Hugging Face.* Rejected as not this: the download
  is announced, sized, cancellable and carries nothing of the person's. It is the one network call
  that is already honest.

## Correction to the finding

**G-03 says "`chat_reading` … is set *after* the request is in flight (`ChatViewModel.kt:78`)".**
Confirmed and slightly sharper than stated: the assignment at `:78` runs after
`Graph.assistant.ask(question, history)` has returned at `:72`, which is the call that **builds the
context and opens the stream**. So the titles are not merely late — they are computed from a request
that has already been sent. There is no window in which the person could have stopped it.

**G-03's "Speech has three explicit disclosure strings … the assistant has none" is right, and
understates the speech side's own gap.** The three speech strings exist and are rendered, but only
in Settings, at the moment of choosing. The screen that actually sends the audio says nothing. That
is why this task adds item 4, which G-03 does not ask for.
